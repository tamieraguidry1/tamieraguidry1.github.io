Selected Work

Case studies from research conducted alongside frontline support work at a shared housing marketplace, including a current collaboration with the product team. All artifacts scrubbed of identifying information.

01

Service Design · Content Strategy · Synthesis

Host Enablement Framework

Mapped 60 days of recurring support ticket patterns into four post-activation friction themes, then designed a layered enablement system aligned to a 90-day host lifecycle. Reduced the gap between platform expectations and host reality at the moment hosts are most likely to churn.

02

Policy Research · Marketplace Design · Trust & Safety

Rebalancing Stakeholder Risk in a Two-Sided Marketplace

A platform's deliberate choice to keep member screening low-barrier left hosts absorbing downstream risk without compensating controls. I built a host-side mitigation framework — tiered deposits, structured pre-booking screening, compliant disclosure standards — that respects the platform's mission while protecting the underserved stakeholder.

Read case study →
03

Decision Tool Design · Policy Translation

Maintenance Response Tier Framework

Hosts oscillated between over-escalating non-urgent issues and under-responding to genuine emergencies. I categorized incidents across two axes — risk to life and risk to property — and produced a four-tier decision tool that gave both hosts and support staff a shared vocabulary for triage.

Read case study →
04

Product Collaboration · Service Design · Voice of Customer

Closing a Notification Gap in Marketplace Rate Changes

Members are auto-notified when a host changes their rate by dashboard, but not when the same change is processed manually through support, leaving them to discover it on their billing. I mapped the asymmetry, weighed its severity honestly, and proposed an automated notification trigger now being scoped with the product team.
